### Inline edit

a click.


![Showcase inline edit feature](https://tableplus.com/resources/images/inline-edit@2x.png)

### Advanced filters

Quickly see the records you need using multiple advanced filters.


![Showcase advanced filters feature](https://tableplus.com/resources/images/advanced-filters@2x.png)

### Code review

Always stay in control of what you have changed on your database.


![Showcase advanced filters feature](https://tableplus.com/resources/images/code-review@2x.png)

![Showcase advanced filters feature](https://tableplus.com/resources/images/warning-mode@2x.png)

anything from your connection.

### Multiple tabs & windows

Working with multiple databases or connections at the same time? Not
a problem.
